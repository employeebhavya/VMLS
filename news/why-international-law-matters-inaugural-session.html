Why International Law Matters

On 6th March 2025, the Centre for the Promotion of International Law successfully hosted the inaugural session of the ‘Why International Law Matters, Especially Today’ series. The event aimed to initiate meaningful discussions on pressing global issues through conversations with distinguished experts in the field of international law.

VMLS | 03rd June, 2024 | 1 min read

The session was led by esteemed speaker Dr. Srinivas Burra, who delivered an insightful presentation on the contemporary relevance of international law. Dr. Burra explored the evolving challenges faced by the global community and highlighted the role of international law in shaping global governance. His presentation provided valuable insights into the complexities and significance of international legal frameworks in addressing current global concerns. The event was co-hosted by Prof. Ananyo Mitra and Prof. Shambhavi Thakur, who emphasized the series' objective of fostering thought-provoking dialogue among students and faculty members. The interactive format of the session encouraged active participation, allowing attendees to engage directly with the speaker and seek clarity on complex legal issues.

The Centre for the Promotion of International Law extends its gratitude to all participants for their involvement and enthusiasm. This inaugural session marked a promising start to the series, and the Centre looks forward to continuing to facilitate impactful discussions on international law in future editions.
