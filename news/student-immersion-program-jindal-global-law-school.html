Student Immersion Program at Jindal Global Law School

From February 6 to February 24, 2025, 33 students from Vinayaka Mission’s Law School (VMLS) participated in a three-week Student Immersion Program at Jindal Global Law School (JGLS), O P Jindal Global University. The program provided a comprehensive and immersive experience of the academic and extracurricular life at JGLS. The student delegation was accompanied by Prof. Abhinandita Biswas and Prof. Ashwani Kumar Singh, who served as the VMLS faculty representatives.

The program featured sessions on a wide range of subjects spanning legal and ancillary disciplines. Students attended informative lectures on topics such as Constitutional Law, Judicial Review of Administrative Action, Environmental Jurisprudence, Flag Jurisprudence, and Fundamentals of Company Law. The sessions were designed to broaden students' understanding of legal principles and their applications. In addition to core law subjects, the program included sessions on interdisciplinary topics such as Economic Behaviour, Cultural Genocide, Mental Health Laws, AI and Legal Learning, and the Reservation Policy vis-à-vis Equality in India. These sessions aimed to equip students with a holistic perspective on the intersection of law and other fields. Several workshops were conducted, enhancing students' practical skills in areas such as Negotiations and Mooting. Furthermore, the program provided students with an introduction to research methodologies and an exploration of the Management-Law Interface.

The students had the opportunity to experience the vibrant culture of Delhi through a one-day trip to iconic landmarks such as Red Fort and Dilli Haat. Overall, the Student Immersion Program at JGLS was a resounding success, offering VMLS students a rich blend of academic learning and cultural experiences.
