Josna M

Assistant Professor

Josna M has more than 3 years of work experience in the library field. Previously she has worked at reputed national level institutions including Indian School of Business, Hyderabad and Indian Institute of Management, Ahmedabad. She has experience in managing all types of library activities.

She has acquainted herself with traditional and modern library activities including Acquisition, Technical Processing, Serial Control, Copyright management, and Electronic Information Services. Her exposure to various databases and other online resources offered by the libraries enabled her to provide effective reference services to the users, including faculty, students, and other user groups.

She has completed her Masters in Library and Information Science from Kannur University, Kerala and has a graduation in B.Sc. Chemistry from Calicut University. She is UGC-NET qualified in Library and Information Science.
