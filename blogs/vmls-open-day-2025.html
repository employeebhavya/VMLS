Career Guidance and Legal Education Insights for Class 12 Students - Admissions Open for 2025-2026

Chennai, May 12, 2025 — Vinayaka Mission’s Law School (VMLS), a forward-looking institution dedicated to transforming legal education in India, invites students and parents to its VMLS Open Day 2025 on Saturday, May 17, 2025, at its state-of-the-art campus in Paiyanur, Chennai. The event aims to offer valuable legal career guidance, information on admission opportunities, and a chance to interact directly with the institution’s leadership and faculty. This initiative is open to all students who have appeared for their Class 12 examinations, including those with pending results or academic backlogs, ensuring that no aspiring learner is left behind.

Event Details:

Event: VMLS Open Day 2025 – Explore Law as a Career
Date: Saturday, May 17, 2025
Time: 9:00 AM onwards
Venue: Vinayaka Nagar, Rajiv Gandhi Salai (OMR), Paiyanur, Chennai – 603104

What to Expect at the VMLS Open Day:

  • Expert career guidance on legal studies
  • Interactive sessions with faculty and student counselors
  • Clarity on admission procedures, eligibility, and course offerings
  • Support and guidance for students with 12th exam backlogs
  • Campus tour and exposure to VMLS’s academic infrastructure

Keynote Address by Dr. Ananth Padmanabhan, Dean, VMLS

A key highlight of the event will be a motivational session by Dr. Ananth Padmanabhan, Dean of VMLS. With academic credentials that span across India’s top law school and leading global universities, Dr. Padmanabhan will speak on the topic:
“What If One Talk Could Shape Your Future?”

His qualifications include:

  • MSx Sloan Fellow, Stanford University
  • LL.M and S.J.D., University of Pennsylvania
  • B.A., LL.B. (Hons.), National Law School of India University, Bengaluru

Dr. Padmanabhan will offer a unique perspective on legal careers in a rapidly changing world and inspire students to view law as a tool for leadership and societal impact.

Academic Mentorship from India’s Leading Law School

Vinayaka Mission’s Law School (VMLS) is proud to be mentored by O.P. Jindal Global University (an Institution of Eminence) and its top-ranked Jindal Global Law School (JGLS). This mentorship ensures that VMLS students benefit from world-class academic standards, research opportunities, and pedagogic innovation rooted in global best practices.

Courses Offered (2025–2026 Academic Year):

  • LL.B. (Hons.)
  • B.B.A. LL.B. (Hons.)
  • B.Com. LL.B. (Hons.)
  • LL.B. (Hons.)
  • LL.B. (General)
  • LL.M.

Each program is crafted to build legal expertise, critical thinking, and professional skills aligned with the needs of today’s legal profession and future challenges in justice, policy, and technology.

The VMLS Open Day is a free-to-attend event, but seats are limited. All students interested in pursuing law, including those currently awaiting Class 12 results or reappearing for subjects, are encouraged to attend.

About Vinayaka Mission’s Law School (VMLS)

Founded with the vision of creating the law school of the future, Vinayaka Mission’s Law School (VMLS) admitted its first cohort in 2021. Since then, it has grown into a vibrant academic hub, driven by innovation, inclusivity, and interdisciplinary learning.

VMLS benefits from institutional mentorship by Jindal Global Law School, India’s finest law school, and a committed team of full-time faculty trained at globally renowned institutions. This academic partnership has enabled pedagogic innovations that equip learners with the knowledge, specializations, and professional readiness needed in the AI-driven legal landscape.

The campus infrastructure includes:

  • Smart classrooms
  • A digitally integrated, well-stocked library
  • A majestic moot court hall
  • Collaborative spaces for immersive legal learning

VMLS takes pride in its commitment to inclusive education, with bilingual academic support to ensure that every learner thrives, regardless of their background.
