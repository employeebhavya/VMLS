Eligibility Criteria to Pursue LLM in Criminal Law and Practice at VMLS

To be able to pursue an LLM in Criminal Law and Practice at VMLS, you must match the eligibility criteria. Firstly, you need to have a bachelor's degree in law (LLB) from a university, or an equivalent institution recognised as deemed to be a university or from a foreign university declared as equivalent to an Indian degree by a competent authority. Satisfying all these mandatory requirements makes you eligible for this course.

Also, candidates who are about to proceed with their final exams can apply; however, they must have their degrees with them during the admission process. If you're eligible as per the requirements, then you'll be able to complete your LLM specialisation in Criminal Law and Practice from the best law university in Chennai and make your resume much stronger and better.

Admission Process for LLM in Criminal Law and Practice at VMLS

To get admitted into the LLM programme in Criminal Law and Practice at VMLS, you need to follow a few easy steps. The university needs your VLAT (VMRF Law Aptitude Test) Score, 10+2 or equivalent marks, and a bachelor's degree in law (LLB) from any university. If you are eligible for admission, then you can get a seat in VMLS in their highly valued LLM specialisation.

The following steps will enable you to know more about the admission process:

  • Register yourself for VLAT, and verify the email address.
  • Fill in the online application form.
  • Pay the required application fees.
  • Apply with all the other required documents.
  • The application will be processed internally.
  • Appear for the VLAT Exam.
  • After the results, you'll get your admission offer letter through the mail.
  • Make sure to complete seat blocking and fee payment.
  • Lastly, you'll get your provisional admission letter.

All these steps will enable you to get admission to the LLM programme in Criminal Law and Practice at VMLS without any issues if all your documents are correct. And, you'll be able to study at the best law school in Tamil Nadu. If you face any difficulties while completing the admission process, then you can contact the team at VMLS, and they will resolve all your doubts quickly.

What are the Various Subjects in this LLM Programme?

The LLM in Criminal Law and Practice will equip you with practical skills and theoretical depth essential for criminal justice reform, policy advocacy, and impactful litigation. It includes core subjects like Criminal Law Theory, Justice and Penal Institutions and Special Criminal Statutes, and Strategic Application that provide a strong conceptual grounding. Hence, it enables you to shape yourself as a great criminal lawyer after completing the programme. The following are the various courses taught in this programme in two different semesters:
