Eligibility Criteria to Pursue LLM in Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technologies, and Intellectual Property at VMLS

To be eligible to pursue an LLM in Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technologies, and Intellectual Property at VMLS, you need to have a bachelor's degree in law (LLB) from a university, or an equivalent institution recognized as deemed to be a university or from a foreign university declared as equivalent to an Indian degree by a competent authority. Satisfying these requirements makes you eligible for this course.

Candidates who are about to proceed with their final exams can also apply; however, they must have their degrees with them during the admission process. If you're eligible as per the requirements, then you'll be able to complete your LLM specialisation in Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technologies, and Intellectual Property from the best law university in Tamil Nadu and strengthen your resume.

Admission Process for LLM in Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technologies, and Intellectual Property at VMLS

The admission process for the LLM in Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technologies, and Intellectual Property programme is quite easy to get admitted to the VMLS. The university needs your VLAT (VMRF Law Aptitude Test) Score, 10+2 or equivalent marks, and a bachelor's degree in law (LLB) from any university. If you are eligible for admission, then you can get a seat in VMLS in their highly valued LLM programme.

The following steps will enable you to know more about the admission process:

  • Register yourself for VLAT, and verify the email address.
  • Fill in the online application form.
  • Pay the required application fees.
  • Apply with all the other required documents.
  • The application will be processed internally.
  • Appear for the VLAT Exam.
  • After the results, you'll get your admission offer letter through the mail.
  • Make sure to complete seat blocking and fee payment.
  • Lastly, you'll get your provisional admission letter.

All these steps will enable you to get admission to the LLM programme at VMLS without any issues if all your documents are correct. And, you'll be able to study at the best law school in Chennai. If you face any difficulties while completing the admission process, then you can contact the team at VMLS, and they will resolve all your doubts quickly.

What are the Different Courses in this Specialization?

The LLM in Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technologies, and Intellectual Property at VMLS helps you step into the future of legal innovation. This specialisation is divided into two semesters and has a total weightage of twenty-seven credits. The program includes the intersection of law, technology, and innovation, equipping you to navigate complex legal landscapes shaped by rapid digital transformation. Subjects like AI, Technology, and Data Protection Laws allow you to tailor your learning to interests like technology law, making you an excellent lawyer.
