All You Need to Know About LLM: Significance, Eligibility Criteria, Admission Process, and Career Opportunities

Law is emerging as a great career in India, and the need for highly qualified lawyers is rising significantly. For this purpose, after completing one's LLB degree, one can opt for the postgraduate LLM program. Law graduates can directly start their career after completing their LLB; however, with an LLM degree from one of the top law colleges in India, one can expect a great start to the career as a lawyer, corporate counsel, public prosecutor, and many more roles.

This informative blog will help you know more about the various LLM programs available at the best law college in India – VMLS, as well as the significance of the LLM degree, the admission process and eligibility criteria at VMLS, and various job opportunities.

What is the Significance of the LLM Programme?

LLM stands for Master of Laws, and it is a highly sought-after postgraduate degree in law. To upskill in your law career, you must complete your LLM degree after completing the LLB degree from a good law school. The LLM degree usually has a duration of one year, where you can choose different specialisations like corporate and financial laws, commercial dispute resolution, criminal law, and many more. Hence, it enables you to align in one direction and kickstart your career as a lawyer with a great academic background.

Law schools like VMLS offer the best-in-class teaching facilities consisting of case studies, visual learning tools, collaborative group projects, in-depth analysis of corporate scandals, personalised dissertation guidance from experts, and many more. Thus, it makes your learning more engaging and transforms your thought process immensely. Also, the programs at VMLS help you to stay ahead in the rapidly evolving legal landscape by exploring emerging technologies like blockchain, AI, fintech, and algorithmic trading.

Eligibility Criteria for the One-Year LLM Programme at VMLS

To be eligible for the one-year LLM law programme, you need to have a bachelor's degree in law (LLB) from a university, or an equivalent institution recognised as deemed to be a university or from a foreign university declared as equivalent to an Indian degree by a competent authority. Candidates who are about to proceed with their final exams can also apply; however, they must have their degrees with them during the admission process. Also, you'll need your VLAT score during the admission process, and then you'll be able to sit for the interview round. If you're eligible as per the requirements, you'll be able to study at the best law college in Tamil Nadu.

VMLS offers an LLM programme in corporate and financial laws, commercial dispute resolution, criminal law and practice, and artificial intelligence, emerging technologies, and intellectual property.

Admission Process for the One-Year LLM Programme

The admission process for the one-year LLM programme is quite straightforward to get admitted to the VMLS. The university needs your VLAT (VMRF Law Aptitude Test) Score, 10+2 or equivalent marks, and a bachelor's degree in law (LLB) from any university. If you are eligible for admission, then you can get a seat in VMLS in their highly valued LLM programme.

The following steps will enable you to know more about the admission process at VMLS:

  • Register yourself for VLAT, and verify the email address.
  • Fill in the online application form.
  • Pay the required application fees.
  • Apply with all the other required documents.
  • The application will be processed internally.
  • Appear for the VLAT Exam.
  • After the results, you'll get your admission offer letter through the mail.
  • Make sure to complete seat blocking and fee payment.
  • Lastly, you'll get your provisional admission letter.
