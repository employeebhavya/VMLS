Dr. Kannan Kunnathully

Assistant Dean - Campus Affairs

Dr. Kannan Kunnathully has recently been awarded a Doctor of Philosophy in Law by Saveetha Institute of Medical and Technical Sciences. His specialization is in International Trade Law and he has done his masters from the University of Wales.

Dr. Kannan brings a wealth of experience, with 7 years in teaching and 18 years of overall professional experience. His strength lies in training students to be industry-ready, equipping them not just for legal practice but also for diverse career opportunities. He has been instrumental in manuscript writing and moot training, helping students refine their research and advocacy skills.

In addition to his academic expertise, he has held key administrative roles, particularly in the disciplinary committee and student affairs. He is passionate about career-building programs, focusing on advocacy skills, social confidence, and overcoming stage fear.

He recently started a new role as the reviewer of articles and Book chapters through online scientific platforms for various journals especially with IGI Global.
