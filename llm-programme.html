LL.M. IN Criminal Law and Practice

Master the nuances of criminal jurisprudence with a specialized LL.M. in Criminal Law and Practice. This program is designed to equip you with theoretical depth and practical skills necessary for impactful litigation, criminal justice reform, and policy advocacy. Core subjects such as Criminal Law Theory, Justice and Penal Institutions and Special Criminal Statutes and Strategic Application provide a strong conceptual grounding. Clinical courses like Advanced Criminal Law Practice (offered across two semesters) emphasize trial techniques, strategic litigation, and post-trial procedures. With cutting-edge electives including 21st Century, Technology, and Criminal Laws and Forensic Law for Lawyers, you will gain exposure to the evolving intersections of law, science, and society. The program also fosters legal research and reform skills through modules on Victimology and Restorative Justice and a dissertation component, while an internship ensures real-world experience. This course prepares you to be a rigorous and responsive criminal law practitioner in today’s complex legal landscape.

LL.M. In Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technologies and Intellectual Property

Step into the future of legal innovation with the LL.M. in Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technologies and Intellectual Property. This forward-looking program explores the intersection of law, technology, and innovation, equipping you to navigate complex legal landscapes shaped by rapid digital transformation. Foundational courses like AI, Technology and Law: Concepts and Challenges and Privacy and Data Protection Law provide critical insights into the legal ramifications of emerging technologies. In the second semester, delve into specialized subjects such as AI and Intellectual Property Law, while elective options allow you to tailor your learning to niche interests in tech law. The curriculum is grounded in research excellence with dedicated modules in legal writing and dissertation development, while practical components like the internship ensure industry exposure. This LL.M. prepares you to become a legal expert at the forefront of regulating and shaping the future of technology and innovation.
